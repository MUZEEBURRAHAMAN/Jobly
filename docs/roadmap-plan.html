Fix first: four problems found this week

Low risk1.2

Darwinbox jobs never arrive

Darwinbox's protection answers 403 Forbidden to every request from GitHub's servers, so the 22 Darwinbox employers added on 2 November show no live jobs (the same requests worked from your Mac). We won't work around a site's block. Do: turn those 22 off in the crawl, ask Darwinbox for a partner feed, and later consider reading them in the user's own browser for companies they follow.

Search matches category names

"product designer" matches every job in the product design category, because search also looks at the category field. Do: search title, company and location only, keep the city aliases (Bengaluru/Bangalore), and rank exact title matches first.

Chip design counted as product design

Jobs such as "CPU Layout Design Engineer" and "IC Design Engineer" land in Product design. Do: send hardware words (IC, ASIC, RTL, layout, silicon, verification) to Engineering before the design rules run, with tests for each.

"New today" overstates

It counts jobs Jobly first saw in the last 24 hours (7,104 today), which jumps whenever we add companies. Do: count jobs posted in the last 24 hours (6,598 today) and say so. The job-card fix from 3 November (stale cards after a search) is done and committed.

One screen for both sign-in and sign-up, like Hira's: "Continue with Google", then "or continue with email" (email and password, with show/hide and a strength hint). Onboarding and LinkedIn import

Low risk1.3

Goal: a complete profile in the first session, because apply assist, match % and resume tailoring all read from it. Target: half of new users at 80% or more on day one.

The flow

  1. Choose a start: Import from LinkedIn (recommended), Upload my resume (PDF or Word), or Fill it in myself.
  2. LinkedIn guide (a small window with three pictures): open your LinkedIn profile, click Resources and then Save to PDF, and drop the downloaded file here. Jobly reads only that file, on your computer.
  3. Review what we found, section by section. Anything uncertain is marked "Check this".
  4. Fill the gaps in short steps: personal, contact, professional, experience, education, skills and links, application answers, resume file.
  5. Finish: "Your profile is 92% complete. 2 things left" with links, and a first look at jobs ranked by match.

Fields, including what Indian forms ask

  • Personal: first and last name, preferred name, pronouns (optional).
  • Contact: email, phone with country code, city, state, country, PIN code, full address (optional; some forms ask).
  • Professional: current title and company, total experience, current CTC and expected CTC (fixed and variable), notice period and last working day, work authorisation by country, sponsorship, relocation, preferred cities, work mode, job types.
  • Experience: title, company, location, dates, current role, bullets.
  • Education: degree, field, institution, dates, CGPA or percentage, with 10th and 12th marks optional (common on Indian forms).
  • Skills and links: skills, languages, LinkedIn, portfolio, GitHub, Behance, Dribbble.
  • Sensitive, optional: gender, disability, veteran status. Default "Prefer not to say", and never answered unless you chose an answer. We never ask for PAN, Aadhaar or passport numbers.
How we build it
pdf.js bundled in the extension; Studio already parses PDFs this way. The LinkedIn PDF has a fixed layout: a left column (Contact, Top Skills, Languages, Certifications) and a right column (name, headline, location, Summary, Experience, Education). We rebuild the columns from text positions and split on those headings, with date ranges like "Jan 2022 - Present (2 years 10 months)". Word files go through mammoth.js. Other resumes use rules first, and AI when it's available (see Resume intelligence).
Files
New lib/import/linkedinPdf.ts, lib/import/resumeParse.ts, components/onboarding/*. The profile schema in lib/profile.ts gains the new fields with migration of existing profiles. Tests use made-up LinkedIn PDFs.
Privacy
No LinkedIn login, no scraping, no upload. The file is read in the browser and only the extracted fields are kept.
Done when
A LinkedIn PDF fills at least 80% of name, headline, experience, education and skills correctly on 10 test files.

Apply assist (auto apply)

Medium risk1.4

The rule: Jobly opens the form, fills it, walks through multi-step forms, and stops before the final Submit. You check it and press Submit. That's Hira's approval step, made concrete. It keeps employer accounts safe, keeps applications good, and keeps us inside Chrome Web Store policy.

The flow

  1. On a job card, Apply with Jobly. Below 60% profile it says "Finish your profile to apply faster" instead.
  2. The listing opens in a tab, and Jobly's side panel opens beside it: "Applying to Senior Product Designer at Zenoti".
  3. Jobly recognises the hiring system and fills the form, resume file included.
  4. The panel groups every question: filled from your profile, written by AI (check it), Action required, and skipped on purpose (demographic questions).
  5. You answer the Action-required items in the panel, ticking "Remember for next time"; Jobly puts them in the form.
  6. On multi-step forms, Next step moves on after you've looked at each step. The last step says "Review and press Submit on the page".
  7. Jobly notices the confirmation page and marks the job Applied, with the date and the answers used.

Fill rules, in order
By share of Jobly's jobs: Workday (45%; multi-step, stable data-automation-id fields; many employers need you to sign in first), Greenhouse (26%; also embedded on company sites in a frame), Ashby (12%), SmartRecruiters (6%), Lever (3%), then Oracle, Keka and Workable. Everything else uses today's generic autofill. Amazon's portal needs its own account, so Apply just opens it.
Answer bank
Questions are normalised (lower case, no punctuation, synonyms such as "notice period" and "how soon can you join") and matched by word overlap, with no AI needed. Answers can be edited and deleted in Settings.
AI
Only for open questions ("Why do you want to work here?"), always labelled, drafted from your profile and the job, and never sent without you seeing it.
Permissions
Adds sidePanel. Filling a tab Jobly opened needs access to that hiring system's domain, so ask for each one as an optional permission the first time (for example *.myworkdayjobs.com, boards.greenhouse.io, jobs.ashbyhq.com). Any other site works from the popup with today's click-to-allow access.
Never
Press Submit, create accounts or type passwords, solve CAPTCHAs, answer demographic questions you didn't answer yourself, or apply in bulk in the background.
Quality
No analytics (privacy promise), so each form gets a "This form didn't fill well" button that opens an email with the page address. Every fill rule gets a test page shaped like the real form.
Done when
On 20 real postings per system, at least 85% of fields fill correctly and no form gets submitted by Jobly.

Resume intelligence

Medium risk1.5

The flow

  1. Import a PDF or Word resume, or start from your profile. Jobly turns it into structured data (the open JSON Resume format), which you can also download and re-import.
  2. Pick a job or paste a job description. Jobly lists what it asks for: must-have skills, nice-to-haves, years, keywords.
  3. See the fit: each requirement as covered or missing, with the line from your resume that covers it, and a keyword coverage score.
  4. Review suggestions as a before-and-after: a rewritten summary, reordered skills, sharpened bullets. Accept or reject each line.
  5. Choose a template, save as PDF or Word, and keep the version linked to that job ("Coverage 62% → 84%").

Rules that keep it honest

  • Every suggested line must point to a fact in your profile; Studio's fact check (guard.ts) removes any that don't.
  • No new employers, titles, dates or numbers. A number not in your profile is flagged.
  • Missing skills are shown as gaps to work on, never added.
  • Nothing changes until you accept it.

Useful even without AI

  • Job-description keyword extraction (dictionary), coverage score, skills reordering, missing-keyword list and templates all work offline.
AI providers
One interface with interchangeable providers: Chrome's built-in on-device model where the computer supports it (free, private; availability is checked at run time), your own key (OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, Gemini), "Claude by hand" (copy a prompt, paste the answer back, as in Studio), and hosted Jobly credits in 2.0. Answers come back as structured JSON.
Code
Move Studio's lib/ai/*, guard.ts and facts.ts into shared/ so the extension and Studio use one engine. Add more ATS-safe templates (single column, plain headings) to the current four.
Done when
Across 20 sample job descriptions, no tailored resume contains a claim missing from the profile, and keyword coverage rises on average.

People finder (Contacts become People)

Changed from the request1.5

Why not an agent that scrapes LinkedIn, Behance, Dribbble and X: LinkedIn's terms forbid automated collection, browser extensions included, and it acts against tools that do it. Building a store of other people's profiles and emails makes us responsible for their data under India's DPDP Act and GDPR. It's also the kind of data collection the Chrome Web Store reviews closely. Jobmate's "send it on LinkedIn" carries the same risk.

The safe version

  1. On a job or company: Find people at Google.
  2. Role-aware searches that open in your own browser: LinkedIn people search ("Product Designer" at Google), a Google search of LinkedIn profiles, Dribbble and Behance search for design roles, GitHub for engineering, X search.
  3. You add the people you choose: name, title, links. Work emails come from Hunter (built in) or Apollo, with your own key.
  4. Each person becomes a card with links, email, status (to contact, contacted, replied) and a referral or outreach draft.

Candidate Intelligence for hiring teams

High riskValidate in weeks 1–8

Verdict: validate now, build only if pilots pay. HireXL shows Indian agencies already pay ₹4,999–14,999 a month for AI screening inside an applicant tracking system, so "AI resume screening" alone isn't a gap. Our opening is narrower and better: evidence-backed shortlists plus useful feedback for rejected candidates, working beside whatever system a company already uses, priced per job.

The first product

  1. Upload a job description and resumes (ZIP, CSV export or forwarded emails).
  2. Jobly extracts the must-haves from the description; the recruiter edits them.
  3. Screening runs in stages: fixed rules, then similarity search, then a careful AI evaluation for the top candidates only.
  4. Each candidate gets an evaluation card: requirements met with the resume lines that prove them, gaps, open questions, confidence, and suggested interview questions.
  5. A ranked shortlist with reasons; the recruiter decides, and nothing is rejected automatically.
  6. Feedback drafts for candidates not selected, reviewed by the recruiter before sending.

How it fits with Jobly

  • Same engine: job-description extraction, resume parsing and fact checks are shared code.
  • Two-sided: with consent, a rejected candidate's feedback can appear in Jobly with "Improve this" links to profile and resume tools.
  • Separate product and brand ("Jobly Hire" as a working name), with separate data storage.

Build, if pilots pay

  • Web app with Postgres and pgvector, encrypted file storage, a job queue, an audit log and isolation per company.
  • A model router: a low-cost model for extraction, a stronger one for evaluation, and the option to self-host open-weight models (Qwen, Gemma, Llama) for personal data.
  • Default retention 90 days, deletion on request, and a data processing agreement.

Unit cost
The earlier estimate (about $3.52 of AI per 1,000 candidates with low-cost models) is an assumption. Measure it on real pilot data before fixing prices.
Payments
Razorpay first for Indian customers (UPI and cards, about 2% plus GST domestic). Stripe later for international cards: Stripe India is invite-only, and about 7.4% of a $20 international payment goes to fees, conversion and GST.
Data and models
Candidate resumes are personal data. Before sending them to any provider, check retention, training and data-location terms. Enterprise buyers will ask, and a provider based in China (such as DeepSeek's API) may be ruled out by some customers.
Law
India's DPDP Act (consent, purpose, deletion rights). The EU AI Act treats candidate screening as high-risk if we serve EU employers (check current dates). New York City requires bias audits for automated hiring tools. Keep a human decision on every candidate, test for unfair patterns, and log the reasons behind every ranking.
Rejection feedback
Companies often avoid detailed rejection reasons for legal reasons, so keep it opt-in, based only on what was actually assessed, reviewed by a recruiter, never revealing other candidates, and with a way for the candidate to flag mistakes.
Validation
Weeks 1–3: 15 interviews (8 agency recruiters, 7 in-house). Weeks 3–6: 3 concierge pilots on consented exports for one role each, comparing our shortlist with the recruiter's picks. Weeks 6–8: put a price in front of them. Go only if 2 of 3 want to pay and agreement with recruiters is at least 70% with no unfair rejections found.
Check first
The survey numbers and LinkedIn Hiring Assistant 2 details in the earlier chat aren't verified; confirm them from the original sources before using them in a pitch. HireXL's results ("8 hours saved per recruiter each week", "45 to 15 days to fill") are its own median claims.
