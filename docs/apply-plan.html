Jobly Autopilot Plan

What applyhira.com does well, what Jobly now does the same way, and the order to build the rest. Jobly runs in the user's browser with no server and no account, so it keeps one rule Hira doesn't: the user always presses Submit.

What applyhira does (looked at on 13 Nov 2026)

  • Roles: one wide search bar, a row of filter pills (sort, posted, location, workplace, companies), a list of row cards (logo, title, "Posted 2h ago", company, place, pay, tags, bookmark, Apply), and the chosen job open in a panel on the right.
  • Applications: a board with In progress, Submitted, Not submitted and Wishlist, a "Needs your input" box, and a daily streak.
  • One application: seven steps (Opening, Reading the form, Filling in, Checking answers, Your review, Sending, Sent), "Nothing is submitted until you approve", and a clear reason when it stops ("Workday hates our agents… finish it on Workday").
  • Knowledge: everything it knows about you, by folder (education, experience, identity, job preferences, projects, skills, work authorization), with where each fact came from.
  • Agents: a chat that sets up job-search agents which watch the board for you.
  • It works in its own cloud browser: it creates accounts on hiring sites with your email and keeps the password.

How the Jobly apply queue works (built in this batch)

  1. 1Opens the job in a background tab
  2. 2Reads the form; presses the page's own Apply link if the form is behind it
  3. 3Fills profile, resume file and saved answers; never overwrites what's there
  4. 4Lists what it couldn't answer under Needs your input
  5. 5Stops. You check the form and press Submit; it goes to Applied

Two tabs are prepared at a time, up to six open. It stops with a reason when there's no form, the form moves to a site Jobly can't open, the site needs a sign-in, or the tab is closed. Answers typed in Jobly are saved for the next form (never gender, ethnicity, disability, veteran status or ID numbers).

Rules that stay

  • The user presses Submit. Jobly fills and stops.
  • No account creation on hiring sites, no stored passwords, no CAPTCHA solving.
  • No copying of another product's code or artwork; the flow above is Jobly's own build.
  • Everything stays on the device; site access is asked for per site.
