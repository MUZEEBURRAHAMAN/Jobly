Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version: Jobly has no accounts and no analytics. Your preferences, saved jobs and hidden companies stay on your device. To show you jobs, the extension asks Jobly's server for them; we don't use those requests to build a profile of you, and we don't sell or share data.

What Jobly is

Jobly is a Chrome extension (and this website) that lists public job postings collected from company career pages. The extension has one purpose: helping you discover and track relevant jobs and apply on the employer's own site.

Information stored on your device

The extension keeps these in your browser's extension storage. They are not uploaded to Jobly's server:

  • Your preferences: roles, experience levels, remote-only, include/exclude keywords, and whether desktop alerts are on.
  • Jobs you save, companies you hide, and a cached copy of recent results so the popup opens instantly and works offline.
  • Your theme choice and whether you have seen the welcome tour. The theme setting uses Chrome's sync storage, so Chrome may sync it across your own devices through your Google account.

Uninstalling the extension removes this data from your device.

Information sent to Jobly's server

When you browse, search or filter, the extension requests jobs from Jobly's server. Each request can include your search text, the filters you chose, and, so the server can find jobs that fit you, your selected roles, experience levels, remote-only setting and keywords. The server uses this only to answer that request and does not store it in a database.

Like any web service, the hosting infrastructure (see below) may keep short-lived technical logs such as your IP address, browser type, time and the requested address. We use them only to keep the service running and secure.

If you use "Report this listing", we store the job, the reason you chose and any note you typed (up to 500 characters). Reports are not linked to your identity.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account details.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • We do not sell or rent data, and we do not use it for credit, lending or unrelated purposes.
  • We do not read the pages you browse. The extension has no access to your tabs or browsing history.

Permissions the extension asks for

  • storage: to keep the items listed above on your device.
  • notifications: to show the optional desktop alert.
  • alarms: to check for new jobs periodically in the background.
  • Access to Jobly's server: to fetch job listings. The extension talks to no other website.

Your choices

  • Turn desktop alerts off in Preferences, or remove saved jobs and hidden companies in the app.
  • Uninstall the extension to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Ask us to delete a report you submitted by contacting us below.

Chrome Web Store user data policy

Jobly's use of information adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Jobly is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for material changes, note it in the extension's release notes.

Contact

Questions or requests: the support contact shown on the Jobly page in the Chrome Web Store.
